How it works

One short loop, every day.

  1. Learn

    Ten new words, five reviews.

    Flashcards with spaced repetition, each word spoken aloud by a recorded voice. Know it on sight and it's learned at once; otherwise it comes back until it sticks.

  2. Translate

    Use them in a real conversation.

    Six-line conversations from everyday life, built only from words you already know. Get four lines right in one go to open the next one.

  3. Your path

    Watch the days add up.

    Daily goal rings, a streak and a monthly learning path. Miss a day and a streak freeze can save it.

  4. Rewards

    Earn titles and creatures.

    Chinese mastery titles and mythic creatures to collect, each one kept a surprise until you earn it.
